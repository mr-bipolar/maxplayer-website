Maxlab Solution built the MaxPlayer app as an ad-supported application. This SERVICE is provided by Maxlab Solution at no cost and is intended for use as is.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and disclosed when you use Max Player.

By using the Service, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information Collection and Use

Max Player does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to use its core media playback features.

Certain information may be stored locally on your device to support app functionality, such as player settings, playlists, favorites, playback history, and other user preferences.

The app also uses third-party services that may collect information to provide their services. Third-party services used include:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob

These services may collect information such as device identifiers, IP address, advertising identifiers, app interactions, and diagnostic information, depending on their configuration and your device settings.

Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy ↗ for more information.

2. Android Permissions and Their Uses

Max Player requests certain Android permissions to provide its features and seamless cross-device playback:

Internet Access INTERNET

Used to access online playlists, live media streams, external links, and advertising services.

Network State ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Used to check network connectivity and help the app manage online playback and network-dependent features.

Foreground Service FOREGROUND_SERVICE

Used to support playback services that need to continue running smoothly when the app is not in the foreground.

Foreground Service – Media Playback FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK

Used to allow continuous audio and video playback in the background where supported by Android.

Notifications POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Used to display media playback notifications and player controls in the notification shade. You can manage notification permissions through your Android device settings.

Camera CAMERA

Used specifically for QR-code scanning to pair your smartphone with Android TV for remote control and text input. Camera access is requested only when the pairing feature requires it.

External Storage WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE

On Android 9 and earlier, this permission is used to save screenshots or other supported files to public storage. On Android 10 and later, Max Player uses Android's modern MediaStore system to save screenshots to the Pictures/MaxPlayer directory without requiring legacy storage permission.

Advertising ID com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID

Used by the Google advertising services integrated into the app to support advertising functionality. Google may use advertising identifiers according to its own privacy policies and user advertising settings.

3. Log Data

In the event of an error while using the Service, information may be collected through third-party services to help diagnose and resolve technical problems. This information may include:

  • Device IP address
  • Device name and hardware model
  • Operating system version
  • App version and configuration
  • Date and time of use
  • Diagnostic and crash information
  • Other technical performance statistics

The information collected depends on the third-party diagnostic services and their configuration.

4. Advertising

Max Player is an ad-supported application and uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

AdMob may collect and process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP addresses, and ad interactions to provide, measure, and improve advertising services.

Depending on your location, consent choices, and advertising configuration, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

For more information, please review Google's advertising information page ↗.

5. Media Playback and MPV Library

Starting from version 2.6, Max Player uses the mpv media player library (mpv) to provide enhanced media playback functionality.

The mpv library operates locally within the app for media playback. It does not independently collect or transmit personal user information.

Media streams and playlists are accessed according to the URLs and sources provided by the user. Those external services may have their own privacy policies and data collection practices.

6. Cookies

Max Player does not directly use cookies as part of its core media player functionality.

However, third-party services and libraries integrated into the app may use cookies or similar technologies as part of their services. You can manage applicable advertising and privacy settings through your device and Google account settings.
